Pilot privacy notice

We store your account, private age eligibility information, profile, friendships, squads, goals, habits, and shared activity content. Audience controls determine who can read each item.

Location starts off. Temporary sharing keeps only your latest point for selected accepted friends. Access ends when you stop or the timer expires. Scheduled cleanup removes expired points. We do not keep route history or sell precise location.

Delete your account from Profile. Safety reports are retained for up to 90 days with deleted reporter accounts unlinked; inbox notifications last 30 days. Backup retention is controlled by the hosting provider.

Community standards

For ages 16+. No harassment, hate, threats, impersonation, exploitation, stalking, or sharing other people’s private information. Report a profile or activity in the app. This is not an emergency service.

Before this pilot is published

The operator must add its legal identity, support email, provider details, and a reviewed privacy policy. Do not publish this placeholder notice as a completed public policy.
